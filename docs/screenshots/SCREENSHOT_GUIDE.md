1. **Log in to HackerRank** first.
2. Click on the direct problem link from the table above.

4. Use `Win + Shift + S` (Windows Snipping Tool) to crop the green `Congratulations!` banner.
5. Save into `docs/screenshots/` with the exact filename shown in the table.
